II   Terms of Acquisition

Private registry request

One artifact. One requester. One transfer window.

Because each Gyre & Wabe artifact is issued as a one-of-one work, acquisitions are handled on a first-come, first-served basis.

Submitting an acquisition request begins the review process for a registered artifact. Approved requests will receive a secure payment link or invoice, along with confirmation of registry transfer once payment is complete.

After approval, the requester will have a 72-hour payment window. If payment is not completed within that window, the acquisition request may be forfeited and the artifact may be offered to the next requester in line.

Gyre & Wabe provides support throughout the process, from request confirmation to payment, fulfillment, and registry status update.
