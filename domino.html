II   Domino Doctrine

Quiet objects. Loud implications.

Domino is the house discipline of order, accusation, and balance.

Gyre & Wabe domino sets are built as functional gaming objects with collector presence. Each set is designed around contrast, rhythm, weight, color discipline, and the visual tension between repetition and singularity.

A domino set may appear calm at first glance. That is usually when it becomes dangerous.

IV   Design Language

The back matters.

The reverse face is where domino becomes object, not inventory.

Domino backs may be kept minimal, metallic, mirrored, opaque, translucent, patterned, or built around a specific house colorway. The best versions do not over-explain themselves. They create tension before the first tile is turned.

Options may include embedded foil, layered pigments, metallic backing concepts, custom contrast, and display-first finishing decisions.

VI   Custom Requests

Commission path

Custom domino begins with restraint, contrast, and a very specific kind of trouble.

Custom requests may include color palettes, backing concepts, metallic effects, opacity level, glitter, foil, ink contrast, display use, gift presentation, or matching dice and chess pieces.

The strongest commissions usually start with a mood rather than a shopping list: casino, funeral parlor, royal argument, mirror room, cursed heirloom, private club, or something worse dressed properly.
