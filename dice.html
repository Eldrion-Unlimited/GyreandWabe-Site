II   Collection Doctrine

The first discipline of the house.

Dice are not stocked. They are issued.

Gyre & Wabe dice are shaped by controlled disorder: resin, pressure, pigment, foil, ink, cure time, restraint, and risk. Each finished set becomes part of a larger house structure rather than a nameless item in inventory.

The dice collection is divided into three paths: High Rollers, Shot Callers, and Open Contract.

V   Open Contract

Commission path

Open Contract is the custom dice path for directed chaos.

Open Contract requests begin with a concept, color direction, visual mood, or intended recipient. From there, the house interprets the commission through resin, fill material, finish, ink, packaging, and presentation.

A contract may request specific colors, foil, glitter, opacity, themes, ink contrast, display treatment, or gift packaging. The final object remains a Gyre & Wabe issue rather than a mass-repeated product.
