II   Collection Doctrine

The dice are the first language of the house.

Each set is shaped by chance, controlled by hand, and recorded by issue.

Gyre & Wabe dice are not treated as interchangeable stock. Each premium set is poured, cured, finished, inked, inspected, photographed, packaged, and assigned a registry identity before acquisition.

The result is a playable object, a display artifact, and a recordable piece of the house archive.

VI   Custom Requests

Commission path

Custom dice begin with color, contrast, intent, and restraint.

Custom requests may include specific color palettes, metallic foil, glitter, themed fills, ink colors, display preferences, and gift packaging. Complex designs may require additional discussion before acceptance.

A custom request does not guarantee duplication. Gyre & Wabe may interpret the requested direction as a singular issue rather than a repeated product.
